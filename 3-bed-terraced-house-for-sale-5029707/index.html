About the property

Description property

REA Stokes and Quirke are delighted to bring to the market this substantial three-bedroom terraced property, ideally positioned on Main Street in the heart of Mullinahone. Presented in good condition throughout, this unique property offers an excellent combination of commercial and residential accommodation, with a former hairdressing salon occupying the ground floor and spacious living accommodation behind and overhead. The ground floor is currently laid out and fitted for use as a hairdressing salon, benefiting from a prominent Main Street position and excellent visibility. The property offers great potential for an owner-occupier wishing to operate a business from the premises, or alternatively as an investment opportunity.

The residential accommodation comprises three well-proportioned bedrooms and two bathrooms, along with comfortable living accommodation. The property benefits from oil-fired central heating and has been well maintained throughout. A particularly impressive feature of this property is the exceptionally large rear garden, offering an abundance of outdoor space and considerable potential for the new owner. Its size is a rarity for such a centrally located Main Street property. Early viewing is advised.
